Cisco Modeling Labs

Getting into the Lab

You reach the lab entirely through your browser, with no VPN and nothing to install. You sign in twice with the same Cisco email: first at the front door, then at CML.

Before you start

  • Your Cisco email — the address Mario added to the access list.
  • The CML password — Mario will share it with you directly, not by email.
1

Open the lab

Go to lab.rooez.com in any browser.

2

Enter your email at the door

Cloudflare will ask for your email. Enter the Cisco address Mario added. If it is not on the list you will see a not authorized page, so confirm with him first if you are unsure.

3

Confirm the one-time code

Cloudflare then emails you a short code. Copy it from your inbox and paste it back into the browser. If it does not arrive within a minute, please check your spam folder.

4

Sign in to CML

You will arrive at the CML login page. Your username is your full email, not just the part before the @. The password is the one Mario gave you.

ⓘ

The Cloudflare sign-in lasts about a day, so your next visit usually goes straight to CML.

Once you are in

You will see the labs that have been shared with you.

Open one, start its nodes, and open a console on any node to begin working. Allow the switches and firewalls a few minutes to finish booting before you expect them to respond.

If something is off

“Not authorized” from Cloudflare
Your email is not on the access list yet. Ask Mario to add it.
The code never arrives
Check spam, then confirm with Mario that he used the right address.
CML rejects your login
The username is your whole email, not the CEC ID alone. If that is right, the password may be off — ask Mario.
No labs are listed
Nothing has been shared with your account yet. Ping Mario.
Trouble getting in? Message Mario Ruiz. The lab runs on a server that is built and torn down per session, so it is not always online — if the front door does not answer, it may be down between sessions.
